# Principles

The direction behind every token and component decision. The full document is
`DESIGN.md` in the repository; this page is the working summary a builder
needs open.

## Who this is for

Pilgrims booking Umrah and Hajj packages - mostly Malaysian, mobile-first,
many making the largest single transaction of their lives, and many for the
first time. The audience skews 30-60 and often reads without reading glasses:
mobile body copy has a hard floor of 15px. Two more audiences share the same
system through separate portals: agency staff (desktop-leaning, all day in
the tool) and platform admins. Navigation differs by portal; nothing else
does.

## Character

**Modern and efficient.** Trust is expressed through competence, not warmth
or ornament. The answer to "this is a sacred journey" is never religious
decoration - no gold gradients, no stacked Kaabah photography. The weight is
carried by restraint and precision: correct Arabic typography, prices that
never wobble, states that are always honest.

Dial: **ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2** - alive but controlled, a
consistent grid with deliberate breaks, transitions on interaction only.

## The five levers

1. **One focal point per screen.** On a package card that is the price; in
   the booking flow, the next action.
2. **One accent, spent once.** Teal is structural - brand, links, primary
   actions. Orange is the accent, reserved for the single most important
   moment on a surface. Never white text on orange-500 (2.82:1, and a build
   contract fails if that ever changes).
3. **Whitespace as structure.** Empty space separates and sets rhythm; it is
   never leftover.
4. **Hierarchical contrast on purpose.** Size, weight and colour differ
   because something is more important, never for variety.
5. **Craft in the boring places.** Aligned price columns, designed empty and
   error states, Arabic sitting correctly beside Malay, visible focus rings.
   If a screen would embarrass us at 2x zoom on a mid-range Android, it is
   not done.

## What the code enforces

These principles are checks, not aspirations: a literal hex, px or duration
in a component stylesheet fails the build; 154 contrast contracts run on
every token build; every story passes axe in a real browser; tabular
numerals are verified against the shipped font files.
